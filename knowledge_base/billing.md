# Billing Support Playbook

## Payment failures

When a customer reports a failed payment, collect the payment attempt time, payment provider reference, checkout session ID, and whether the bank account or card was charged.

Never ask for full card numbers, CVV values, or banking passwords. Direct the customer to the secure billing portal for payment method updates.

If money was debited but an order failed, acknowledge the issue, verify provider status, and escalate to billing operations for reconciliation.

## Refunds

Refund requests should include order ID, transaction ID, customer email, and reason. High-value or duplicate-charge refunds require human review before confirmation.
